# Account Deletion — Bonim (בונים – ניהול אתרי בנייה)

**App:** בונים – ניהול אתרי בנייה
**Package ID:** `com.rachelelyashiv.contractorapp`

This page explains how to delete your account and its associated data.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **rachelel058@gmail.com**
2. Use the subject line: **Account deletion request**
3. In the message, state the **email address you registered with**

Send the request from the address on the account, or we will ask you to verify ownership another way, so that we can confirm the request comes from the account holder.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of the following:

- Account details — name, email address, password, phone number, company name and profile picture
- Every project and apartment you created
- Workers and their attendance records
- Materials and stock
- Invoices and expenses
- All photos and documents you uploaded

## What is kept, and for how long

Once the request is confirmed, the data is deleted within **30 days**.

We keep no copy of your data after deletion. Server operational logs may retain technical identifiers for a short further period until they rotate; these cannot be tied back to the contents of the account.
